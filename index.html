HOW IT WORKS — IT'S A BIRDIE

Simple as a tap-in. Here's how the GSPro bounty works.

🏌️ GOLFER SCORECARD
🏌️
HOLE 1 — Post Your Bounty
Describe your dream course. Name it, share some details, and set your budget. Go solo or rally your simulator crew — everyone chips in, everyone plays.

Designers pitch with portfolios, timelines, and pricing. Browse their work, pick your builder, and lock it in.

HOLE 3 — Get Paid
Complete the course, pass client review, collect payment. CourseBounty handles the escrow so you're always protected.
